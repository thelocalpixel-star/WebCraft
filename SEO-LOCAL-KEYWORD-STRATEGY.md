# WebCraft local keyword strategy — November 6, 2026

## Decision

Target local hiring intent for each of the five services. Give Cleveland and Chattanooga the first content and measurement attention, retain nearby communities and Knoxville as the next wave, and keep all 24 existing locations in the candidate map. Do not spend equal effort on all terms or create a page for every phrase.

The earlier map tested only five exact phrases per city. That missed common wording such as “SEO Chattanooga,” “website designer,” “website redesign” and “PPC management.” The new universe contains 768 candidate phrases: 32 per community. It is a structured hypothesis list based on actual offerings, not 768 measured keyword opportunities.

Files:

- `SEO-LOCAL-KEYWORD-UNIVERSE.csv`: 768 unique commercial candidates, service clusters, market tiers, business priority and proposed destinations.
- `SEO-KEYWORD-EVIDENCE.csv`: 17 observed provider metric rows, including 12 from earlier today and five newly observed variants or broader terms. Only recorded exact phrases receive those values.
- `SEO-INITIAL-MEASUREMENT-SET.csv`: 36 deliberate initial tracking candidates across six priority cities. No scheduled tracker, recurring credit spend or saved-keyword tagging was created.
- `SEO-KEYWORD-MAP.csv`: earlier 120-phrase seed experiment retained for provenance. The universe is the newer planning map.

## The 32 local phrase families

Append a real community and its state, for example “website designer Cleveland TN” or “PPC management Chattanooga TN.” Close variants such as “Chattanooga web design” belong to the same intent cluster. Do not write repetitive city/state strings into every sentence.

| Service | Core phrases | Secondary phrases | Scope condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Web design | web design; website design; website designer; small business web design | affordable web design; custom website design; website redesign; small business website design | Standard informational websites; do not imply unsupported ecommerce or app development |
| Hosting and care | website hosting; website maintenance | web hosting; small business website hosting; website care plans | Confirm platform compatibility, included care and update limits |
| Local SEO | local SEO; SEO services | SEO company; small business SEO; local search optimization; Google Business Profile optimization; Google Maps optimization | Business Profile/Maps terms are conditional on actual deliverables and Google's eligibility rules |
| Google Ads | Google Ads management; PPC management | Google Ads agency; Google AdWords management; paid search management; Google Ads consultant | Paid search only; do not expand into unoffered social or display-ad services |
| Analytics | Google Analytics setup; conversion tracking setup | GA4 setup; Google Analytics consultant; website analytics services; Google Ads conversion tracking | Specialized inquiry intent; local demand often unmeasured |

Suggested secondary word order variants: “[city] web design,” “[city] website designer,” “SEO [city],” “[city] SEO company,” “[city] PPC management.” Keep these as synonyms of the same page target. Use TN for Cleveland, Athens and Dayton to reduce wrong-state traffic. Do not pursue “Chattnooga” as a separate typo page.

## Page ownership and execution

For a query naming a city, the proposed primary destination is that city's existing page. It should answer how the relevant service is delivered there and link to the detailed service page. The central service pages own the broader service offer and regional intent. This is a proposed ownership model, not a statement that Google currently ranks these pages.

This differs from the earlier seed map, which sent most local non-design phrases directly to generic service pages. The revised approach gives local hiring queries a location-aware destination while retaining the service detail needed to make a buying decision. Search Console should determine whether that division works in practice.

1. On each priority city page, explain the actual scope of web design, hosting/care, SEO, Ads and measurement in useful sections. Include quote boundaries, account ownership, delivery process and related buyer guides. Do not repeat the same long blocks across every city.
2. Strengthen the core city pages with genuine project examples, business identity and customer evidence when available. Current fictional design concepts cannot serve as client proof.
3. Add natural contextual links between service pages, city pages and guides. Existing service-area directories already provide discovery paths.
4. Split a service-city page only when search results indicate a distinct need and WebCraft has enough unique scope, experience or examples to answer it. A 24-by-five doorway grid is not the plan.
5. Start measurement with 36 phrases across Cleveland, Chattanooga, Athens, Ooltewah, Dayton and Knoxville. Use location-controlled organic checks and eligible local-pack checks separately. Expand the measurement list from real queries, not every permutation.

## Exclusions

Reject jobs, salaries, courses, certification searches, software logins, Google Ads account support, free website builders, unrelated cities/states and competitors' brand names. Avoid cheap hosting comparisons if the visitor wants commodity server plans rather than WebCraft's hosting/care offer. Do not target ecommerce builds, WordPress repair, Shopify, social-media management or other platform/service terms until actual capability and offer scope are confirmed.

“Near me” should be monitored through local search data where useful, not repeated in page titles or used to imply a nearby office. “Best” is not a supportable business claim without evidence. Search volume does not outweigh relevance to paying customers.

## Measurement and limits

Baseline from earlier today: Search Console page data August 3–November 3 returned four homepage impressions, zero clicks and no other page rows. Query rows were unavailable. This is too little first-party evidence to claim which service/location keywords already work. Query text naming a city does not reveal the searcher's physical city.

Review indexed pages, nonbranded impressions, clicks, query-to-page matches and qualified inquiries over matching periods. Do not call a term a failure while its page is unindexed or reports are incomplete. The confirmed WebCraft form event has been tested in GA4, but a successful form is still different from a qualified lead.

No new page, ranking claim or scheduled paid rank tracker was created during this research. This pass used the marketing content-strategy and SEO guidance with the connected OpenSEO browser UI. The optional OpenSEO skill files/tools were not exposed in this turn; the previously installed skill directory is now absent. Existing earlier metric results were reused instead of re-purchasing the full seed batch.
