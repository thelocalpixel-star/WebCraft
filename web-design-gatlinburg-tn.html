Serving Gatlinburg and Sevier County

Gatlinburg, at the door of the Smokies.

Gatlinburg is a mountain resort town in Sevier County, whose county seat is Sevierville, at the edge of Great Smoky Mountains National Park. U.S. Route 441 — the Parkway — runs through town and becomes Newfound Gap Road into the park.

The Sugarlands Visitor Center sits two miles south of town, and Anakeesta mountaintop adventure park, Ripley’s Aquarium of the Smokies and the Roaring Fork Motor Nature Trail, with its preserved log cabins and waterfalls, line the Parkway. Kuwohi, at 6,643 feet, is the highest point in Tennessee.

Great Smoky Mountains National Park charges no entrance fee, though vehicles parked more than 15 minutes need a parking tag. Newfound Gap, at 5,046 feet, is the lowest drivable gap through the park, on the Tennessee–North Carolina line.

We serve businesses in Gatlinburg and across Sevier County, including Pigeon Forge and Sevierville, as well as nearby Cherokee, North Carolina. Work is done remotely by phone, email and online project reviews; there is no separate Gatlinburg office.
