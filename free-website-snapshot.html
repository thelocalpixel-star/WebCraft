Free website snapshot

A free snapshot of where your website stands.

Search has changed. People ask Google, ChatGPT and Perplexity, and the answers quote the pages they trust. The snapshot is a by-hand check of how those systems see your website — what is holding it back, what to fix first, and what is already working.

What you get

Five checks, in priority order — the blockers first, then the quick wins, then the content gaps:

Technical setup. Whether the site loads cleanly, can be crawled, and has no broken pages or missing basics.

Content. Whether each page states clearly what you sell, who it is for, and what to do next.

Local visibility. Whether your business details are consistent and your service area is clear.

AI and schema signals. Whether machines can read the site — structured data, direct answers, and FAQs.

Trust and proof. What a visitor, or an AI answer, can verify about you — and what is missing.

Who it is for

This is for a small business that already has a website and a sense that it should be doing more — or no website yet and a wish to start from a clear picture. It is most useful if you are ready to act on what it finds.

How it works

1. Send your details. Your website address, the city you serve, and what you sell — through the contact form on the homepage, choosing "Free website snapshot" in the list.

2. We look at the site by hand. No automated report, no generated score. A person reads the pages the way a customer and a search engine would.

3. We reply personally. You get the blockers in priority order, the quick wins, and the content gaps — in plain words, with what to do next.

What happens after

That is up to you. Implement the fixes yourself, bring the list to whoever runs your website, or ask us what the work would cost. A website is $500 one-time, with hosting and care optional at $20 a month. There is no obligation and no follow-up pitch.
