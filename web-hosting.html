How your web hosting project starts

Review the current setup

Share the website address and platform, hosting renewal details and the services that depend on your domain. Use account invitations where available instead of sending passwords.

Confirm SSL setup, update requests, backup arrangements, recovery responsibilities and support expectations in the scope. A hosting plan and unlimited website development are different commitments.

Record the current domain settings and agree on the changeover. Check the website and relevant contact paths after the move, and keep account ownership clear.

Know who looks after your website after launch.

Your domain is the address people type; hosting is where your website is served. Before choosing a plan, make sure you know who owns each account, when renewals happen and how to get access if you change providers.

Optional hosting and care for the standard WebCraft website package is $20 per month. Domain registrations, third-party subscriptions and work outside the agreed care scope are separate. Ask what is covered before moving an existing website.

For an existing site, we first need its platform, current host, domain provider and any connected email or booking tools. A move should account for those dependencies so a website change does not accidentally interrupt another service.

What the $20 monthly care covers — and what hosting alone does not.

Hosting alone keeps a website served and reachable. It does not write new content, update hours, fix a broken form or answer a question about the site. WebCraft’s optional $20 monthly hosting and care combines the two: the hosting that serves the standard website package, plus agreed care for the site itself.

Care covers small content updates within the agreed scope — revised hours, a changed service description, a new photo you supply — and support for the website WebCraft built. It does not cover a redesign, new pages beyond the agreed scope, or work on a platform WebCraft did not build. Extra pages are $50 each, agreed separately.

Before comparing a hosting-only price with a hosting-and-care price, compare what each one includes. A lower monthly figure that covers only the server is a different purchase from a monthly figure that also covers someone looking after the site. Ask any provider to list, in writing, what the monthly price covers and what it does not.

Web hosting: questions before you start

Can you host a website built elsewhere?

We need to review its platform and requirements first. Share the URL and current setup so we can confirm compatibility and quote any migration work. Not every platform can move as a set of website files.

Does the $20 monthly price include a domain?

Domain and third-party fees are separate from the optional hosting and care price for the standard WebCraft website package. Confirm renewal costs and account ownership before you choose a plan.

Will changing hosting affect business email?

It can if domain records used for email are changed. Tell us which email provider you use so the website move can account for those records. Website hosting does not automatically include email hosting.

What happens if I want to move later?

The standard website package includes full website file ownership. Confirm the domain owner, account access and any third-party dependencies in your scope so a future handoff is understood.

What does the $20 monthly care cover that hosting alone does not?

Hosting alone serves the website files. The optional $20 monthly hosting and care also covers small content updates within the agreed scope and support for your WebCraft website. Redesigns, new pages beyond the agreed scope, and third-party fees are separate.
