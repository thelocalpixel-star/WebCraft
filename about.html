Four steps. No surprises.

You always know what happens next, what it costs, and who to call.

1. Tell me what the website needs to do.A call, a quote request, a booking — the pages are chosen around that job, not around a template.

2. Get a quote in writing.Price, pages and scope agreed before work starts. 50% to start, 50% when the site is live.

3. Review a draft.You see the website online and check it on a phone. Changes by call or in writing.

4. Launch — and it’s yours.The site goes live with your login details, file list and editing guide. Website, domain and files: yours.

Why flat?

Because you should know the price before the work starts, not after. The package is the same for every small business website up to five pages: custom design, mobile layouts, a contact form, full file ownership. One price, agreed in writing, before work starts.

What costs extra is named in the quote — extra pages at $50 each, hosting and care at $20 a month if you want it, and anything outside the package, like online payments or booking systems, scoped separately before you commit.
