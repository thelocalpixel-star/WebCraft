About WebCraft

About WebCraft — Mike Stephen

WebCraft is a one-person web design business run by Mike Stephen, 32, based near Cleveland, Tennessee. It builds websites for small businesses across an approximate 200-mile service area — Cleveland, Chattanooga, Athens, Knoxville and the surrounding region — working remotely by phone, email and online draft reviews.

What WebCraft does

Five services, for small businesses that need a website that brings in work: web design, web hosting, local SEO, Google Ads and analytics. A website is $500 one time, for up to five pages, with a custom design, mobile layouts, a contact form and full file ownership. Extra pages are $50 each. Hosting and care is $20 a month, optional. Local SEO, Google Ads and analytics are quoted around the market — there is no fixed price for ongoing work, and no ranking guarantee.

There is no separate office in each city. Projects run remotely, the same way for every town in the service area: you talk to the person who builds the website, you review a draft online, and you check it on a phone before it goes live.

How a project works

1. Tell us what the website needs to do. A call, a quote request, a booking — the pages are chosen around that job, not around a template.

2. Get a quote in writing. The price, the pages and the scope are agreed before work starts. You pay 50% to start and 50% when the site is live.

3. Review a draft. You see the website online and check it on a phone. Changes are discussed on a call or in writing.

4. Launch and handoff. The site goes live, and you get the login details, the file list and an editing guide — the website, the domain and the files are yours.

Why the price is flat

The price is $500 because the package is the same for every small business website up to five pages: a custom design, mobile layouts, a contact form and full file ownership. There is no hourly billing and no quote that changes after the sales call — the price, the pages and the scope are in writing before work starts. What costs extra is named in the quote: extra pages at $50 each, hosting and care at $20 a month if you want it, and anything outside the package — online payments, booking systems and other integrations — scoped separately before you commit.
