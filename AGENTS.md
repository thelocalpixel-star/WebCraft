# WebCraft project guidance

WebCraft offers web design, web hosting, local SEO, Google Ads and analytics for small businesses. The current goal is to improve qualified local search visibility and inquiries within roughly 200 miles of Cleveland, Tennessee, including Cleveland, Chattanooga, Athens, Ooltewah, and Dayton. Treat the service-area radius as approximate; do not imply physical offices in each community. Knoxville is within the expanded service area.

For SEO changes, consult current Google Search Central guidance and relevant available SEO and copywriting skills. Favor useful, original customer-facing content and accurate local facts over keyword stuffing or duplicate city pages. Never invent customer reviews, project outcomes, business locations, or rankings. Keep the sitemap, canonical URLs, and Google Analytics tracking consistent when pages change. Verify indexing using Search Console when access is available; do not promise indexing or rankings.
