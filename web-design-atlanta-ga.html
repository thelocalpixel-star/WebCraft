Serving Atlanta and Fulton County

Web design for Atlanta businesses, in the seat of Fulton County.

Atlanta is the county seat of Fulton County and the capital of Georgia. Three interstates converge on the city: I-20 runs east–west, while I-75 and I-85 run north–south and join as the Downtown Connector through the center, and I-285, the Perimeter, loops around the city.

Downtown, Midtown and Buckhead are the city’s main business districts, along Peachtree Street and the Connector, and Hartsfield–Jackson Atlanta International Airport sits about 10 miles south of downtown. Locals describe places as inside or outside the Perimeter — ITP or OTP.
