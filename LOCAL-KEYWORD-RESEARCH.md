Scope update: the owner expanded coverage to approximately 200 miles and added hosting, local SEO, Google Ads and analytics. The research below records the earlier five-city campaign; use AGENTS.md for current coverage and WEBSITE-REVIEW.md for the implemented expansion. Knoxville is now in scope.

Research date: November 4, 2026. Market: approximately 60 miles from Cleveland, Tennessee; focus on Cleveland, Chattanooga, Athens, Ooltewah and Dayton. Knoxville is outside this campaign.

## Evidence and limits

This is qualitative keyword research based on live web search results, direct competitor pages, current Google guidance, and the site's existing content. It is not a location-controlled Google rank report. No paid keyword database or Google Ads Keyword Planner volume data was available. Monthly searches, keyword difficulty, current WebCraft positions and traffic forecasts are not measured. Proposed article phrases are keyword hypotheses; validate with Search Console impressions after publication and indexing. Search results do not establish that any keyword is easy.

Searches sampled included web design Cleveland TN small business website; web design Chattanooga TN small business website; web design Athens TN; web design Ooltewah; web design Dayton TN; website cost Cleveland TN; and local website cost/redesign combinations. Always retain Tennessee or TN to avoid Cleveland, Ohio; Athens, Georgia; and Dayton, Ohio.

Observations from first-party competitor pages:

- Sundae Websites presents Cleveland web design, a small-site monthly offer, portfolio work and separate service/location navigation: https://sundaewebsites.com/
- White Oak Design has a dedicated Chattanooga service page, published five-page pricing and supporting process articles: https://www.whiteoakdesign.com/chattanooga-web-design.html
- Trueline Web Co. presents Athens positioning, project examples, costs, ownership and a buying process: https://truelinewebco.com/
- NoogaBytes presents Chattanooga redesign services with a real project narrative and separate tune-up/rebuild scopes: https://noogabytes.com/websites
- Ooltewah results included local provider listings and Yeet Websites, which publishes cost and ownership information: https://yeetwebsites.com/
- Dayton's sample was sparse and included business directories. This is insufficient evidence to call Dayton low competition or assign search demand.

Inference: clear pricing, credible project detail and an understandable buying process are useful competitive requirements. Low price alone is not unique. Commercial queries in the sample returned provider/service pages; articles should support those pages rather than replace them.

## Publishing and measurement

- Put the topic naturally in title, H1 and introduction. Write for the customer; no keyword-density targets. Image alt text describes the image.
- Assign one main URL per intent; use natural related phrases on that page. Do not multiply every article by five cities.
- Include original examples, a real author or business byline, references where needed and accurate publication/update dates.
- Add relevant contextual internal links, not a repeated list of cities in every paragraph.
- New public pages need self-referencing canonicals, GA4 G-4GJ9T13HPP and inclusion in sitemap.xml. Update lastmod only for actual substantive changes. Research documents are not public website pages and do not belong in the sitemap.
- Verify HTTP success, robots/indexability, mobile layout, links and forms. Keep crawl requests distinct from confirmed indexing.
- Track Search Console query groups and destination pages for each city, using TN variants where appropriate. Query text containing a city is not the searcher's city location.
- Track GA4 organic landing pages, successful inquiry submissions, email/phone clicks and qualified leads. Existing page-view tags alone do not establish successful lead tracking. Never send contact-field values to analytics.
- Review weekly for crawl errors and discovery; evaluate content impressions/clicks over 28-day windows after indexing. No impressions while unindexed is not evidence of no keyword demand. If indexed pages gain no relevant impressions, reassess topic fit, substance, internal links and competition before producing more pages. If impressions grow but inquiries do not, inspect intent, offer clarity and form usability.
