Start with one service, a defined area and a measurable inquiry.

Before a campaign starts, choose the service you want to sell and the customer action that matters. A quote request, a confirmed booking and a click on a phone number are different signals. The reporting should make that distinction clear.

Campaign work can include search-keyword planning, negative keywords, ad copy, location settings and landing-page recommendations. We review the terms that actually trigger ads so irrelevant searches can be identified and addressed.

Advertising spend is paid separately to Google. Management fees and any landing-page or tracking work are separately agreed. We do not estimate a useful budget from a city name alone; competition, service value, search demand and your ability to follow up all matter.

Google Ads: questions before you start

Is Google Ads spend included in website pricing?

No. Advertising spend, campaign management and the website package are separate costs. We agree on the campaign scope and budget before launch.

Can you target Cleveland, Chattanooga or smaller communities?

Campaigns can be planned around your real service area. Location options and available reach need to be reviewed in the account; targeting is not proof that every person seeing an ad is physically inside a boundary.

Should I choose SEO or Google Ads first?

It depends on the offer, budget, website readiness and how quickly you need to test demand. SEO develops organic visibility over time. Ads buy exposure while a campaign runs. Both need an effective inquiry path.

Will you guarantee a cost per lead?

No. Costs and lead quality depend on search demand, competition, the offer and follow-up. We agree on measurement and review criteria instead of guaranteeing the same cost for every business.
