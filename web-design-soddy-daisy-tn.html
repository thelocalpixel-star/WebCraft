Serving Soddy-Daisy and Hamilton County

Soddy-Daisy, on Chickamauga Lake.

Soddy-Daisy is a city in northern Hamilton County, whose county seat is Chattanooga, on Chickamauga Lake about 20 minutes north of downtown Chattanooga. U.S. Route 27 runs through town, with Dayton Pike alongside it.

Chickamauga Lake, a 36,000-acre TVA reservoir on the Tennessee River, borders the city’s eastern side, and Soddy Lake, a calm embayment inside the town limits, is a local favorite for boating and fishing. Big Soddy Creek Gulf is a 285-acre park, and North Chickamauga Creek Gorge State Park cuts through Walden Ridge nearby.

Poe’s Tavern, built in 1819, was Hamilton County’s first courthouse. The Soddy-Daisy Heritage Museum sits in the historic 1921 Soddy Bank Building, and Harrison Bay State Park on Chickamauga Lake is just down the road.

We serve businesses across northern Hamilton County, including Dayton, Sale Creek and Graysville, as well as nearby Hixson. Work is done remotely by phone, email and online project reviews; there is no separate Soddy-Daisy office.
