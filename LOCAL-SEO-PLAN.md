# WebCraft local search plan

## Indexing

The public sitemap is https://localwebcraft.com/sitemap.xml. Submit it to the verified domain property in Google Search Console. Use URL Inspection to inspect and, where appropriate, request indexing for https://localwebcraft.com/ and https://localwebcraft.com/webcraft-ad.html. A successful submission is not a guarantee of indexing.

## Measure search and leads

Every 28 days, use the Search Console Search results report to filter queries containing Cleveland, Chattanooga, Athens and Knoxville separately. Record clicks, impressions, CTR and average position, alongside total clicks. These are city-name searches, not searches originating in each city: Search Console reports countries, not city location. Query anonymization may omit some searches.

The homepage contact form includes optional city and referral-source fields in the submitted inquiry. Referral source is self-reported and can help identify inquiries from Google or recommendations. Count received inquiries by that field, including an unknown/other bucket. This tracks form leads only, not calls or email clicks, and it does not attribute a lead to an individual search. Once a Google Analytics account and measurement ID are available, configure privacy-conscious conversion events for successful form submissions, email and phone clicks; distinguish submissions from attempts and avoid sending names, email addresses, or messages to analytics.

Suggested monthly report: month | market or city-name query | impressions | clicks | CTR | average position | inquiries received (self-reported city) | qualified leads. Do not treat empty or delayed Search Console data as zero demand.

## Honest reviews

Request feedback from actual customers only, without payment, discounts or pressure. Example: “Thanks for working with WebCraft. If you’d like to share your experience, here’s our Google review link: [verified review link]. Honest feedback helps other local businesses decide.” Send only with the owner’s permission and after confirming the correct profile and customer details.
