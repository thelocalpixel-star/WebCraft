# Location content and keyword implementation — November 4, 2026

## What this research establishes

Live search queries were run for all 24 featured city/state names using web design, small-business websites, pricing and SEO terms. Results were screened for locality and relevance: similarly named cities and national website-builder articles were excluded from competitor conclusions. Broad web search results are not a location-controlled Google rank report. Several small-market samples returned regional or irrelevant results; this is not evidence of low competition or demand.

No verified search-volume, keyword-difficulty or ranking figures were available. The article keywords below are editorial hypotheses selected for business relevance and distinct informational intent, not proven high-volume keywords. Commercial web-design and marketing queries remain assigned to location/service pages. Articles support buyer questions and link back to those pages.

## Quality and measurement

The 20 new articles answer different questions; four existing city-specific guides are retained. Regional guides also remain available as broader resources. These articles provide planning advice for each intended city audience, without claiming the business buying process is geographically unique. No local events, statistics, offices, testimonials or outcomes were invented. The new abstract image is decorative brand artwork, not evidence of a client result or city photograph.

After deployment, check indexing before treating absent impressions as weak demand. Review query and page impressions over meaningful windows, then qualified inquiries. A city in a query is not the searcher's city. Search Console currently reports a daily individual-request quota; sitemap submission and published URLs do not establish indexing. Do not repeat quota-blocked requests today.

## Verification

65 pages passed HTTP, canonical, heading, internal link/anchor, asset, JSON-LD, GA4 and sitemap checks. Browser checks at 320, 390, 768 and 1440 pixels covered all 65 pages (260 page-size combinations): no detected horizontal overflow, broken loaded images, missing H1 or visible anti-spam control. Reciprocal city/article links and replacement hero images verified across all 24 locations. Mobile city hero and article opening visually reviewed. Tests do not establish indexing or rankings.
