Analytics: questions before you start

What is the difference between GA4 and Search Console?

Search Console describes visibility and clicks from Google Search. GA4 describes measured activity on your website. They use different methods and do not report identical totals.

Can you measure contact forms and phone calls?

We can plan tracking around confirmed form submissions and phone-link clicks. A phone-link click does not prove a call connected. Measuring answered calls or qualified leads may require additional tools or records.

Can you fix duplicate or missing events?

We can review the tags, success signals and integrations within the agreed access. Diagnosis comes first because a server integration can send events that are not visible in the website code.

Does tracking send customer messages to Google?

A tracking plan should avoid sending names, email addresses, phone numbers or message text as analytics event parameters. The reporting should describe the action and page rather than copy the inquiry’s contents.
