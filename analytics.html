Yes. We check what triggers each event and test successful and failed form submissions.

What is the difference between GA4 and Search Console?

GA4 tracks activity on your site. Search Console shows how the site appears and performs in Google Search.

We configure measurement to track actions without sending form contents or personal contact details.
