Match the page to the way customers buy

A restaurant may need a current menu, hours and directions. A contractor may benefit from service descriptions, project photos and a quote request. A professional service can explain who it helps and what happens in an initial consultation. Prioritize the action your customers actually take.

Keep information consistent

Review the business name, phone number and service details on your website and any listings you manage. If you have an eligible Google Business Profile, keep it accurate and follow Google’s rules for locations and service areas. A listing does not replace a website where you can explain your offer in depth.
