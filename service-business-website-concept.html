The thinking behind the page

Help a homeowner decide what to ask for.

Describe the work before the pitch

Separate lawn care, planting and cleanups so a homeowner can identify the relevant service. A real project would use the business’s actual service limits, photos and availability.

Keep the estimate request focused

Ask for the service, property area and a short description. Confirm delivery before showing a success message. This concept does not collect customer information.

Show evidence the business can supply

A real build could include approved project photos and attributed customer feedback. The evidence and wording would be reviewed with the business before publication.

An example page plan

Five pages with a job to do.

Home introduces the business. Services explains the work. Work shows approved project photos. About introduces the people. Contact makes the estimate request clear.
