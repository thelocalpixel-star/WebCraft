Choose the right information for the job

If you run a local shop, show current hours and explain what customers can expect when they visit. If you provide services at customers’ properties, list the jobs you take and the places you serve. A frequently asked questions section can address the details you explain over and over.

Prepare the essentials before design starts

Collect your logo, current contact details and photos you own or have permission to use. Write down your top services and the action you want visitors to take. A focused list makes it easier to decide which pages your business needs.
