Domains, hosting and website ownership: what to ask before you sign

Your website’s address, hosting account and files serve different purposes. Knowing who controls each one helps you avoid surprises when it is time to make a change.

Your domain is your address

A domain such as example.com is registered through a domain registrar. Ask whose account holds it, who pays for renewal, and who can change DNS settings. Keep your contact information current so renewal notices reach you.

Hosting makes the site available

A host serves your website to visitors. Ask whether hosting is included, what recurring charges apply, and who handles backups, security updates and outages. Some sites need additional services, such as a form provider or booking tool.

Clarify ownership and access

Before work begins, ask what you will receive at handoff: source files, access to the domain, content, images and any third-party accounts. Confirm whether ongoing changes are included or billed separately, and what happens if you decide to move.
