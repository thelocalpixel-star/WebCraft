A guide for local business owners

Contractor website checklist: turn local searches into conversations

For contractors in Cleveland, Chattanooga, Athens and Ooltewah, a website should answer two questions quickly: can you handle this job, and how can I contact you?

Explain the jobs you actually take

List your core services in plain language. A grading contractor might separate excavation, clearing, drainage and hardscapes; a roofer might explain repairs, replacement and inspection. Say where you work and avoid listing services you do not provide.

Show the work clearly

With the customer’s permission, add project photos with short descriptions: the work performed, the general area and the relevant service. Avoid claiming a project outcome or performance result you cannot verify. Our GradeWorks example shows how service descriptions and a project gallery can sit together.

Make estimates easy to request

Give visitors a phone link and a simple form. Ask for the job location, service needed and a short description; collect only what you need to respond. Explain your expected response time and keep it accurate.

Prepare before you launch

Check the page on a phone, test the call button and form, confirm your coverage area, and check all license or insurance statements for accuracy. Keep those details current when the business changes.
