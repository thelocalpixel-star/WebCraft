A guide for local business owners

What to prepare before your business website is built

A little preparation helps a website project move smoothly. Gather the information a new customer needs and share it with your designer before the first draft.

Start with your customer’s questions

Write a short description of what you do, who you help, where you work, and what someone should do next. List the services you want customers to ask about. If your hours or service areas vary, write down the current details.

Collect photos you can use

Gather your logo and original photographs of your team, work, products or location. Confirm you have permission to use each image and any identifiable customer projects. A few clear, relevant images are more useful than a large folder of unrelated stock photos.

Bring accurate contact details

Choose the phone number and email you monitor, and provide your public address only if customers actually visit there. Tell your designer what information you need from an inquiry and how quickly you can respond.

Plan the review and handoff

Choose one person to collect feedback on the draft. Before launch, test links and forms on mobile, check spelling, and confirm who controls the domain, hosting, files and any third-party services. After launch, keep hours and services current.
