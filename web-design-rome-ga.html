Serving Rome and Floyd County

Rome, between the rivers in Floyd County.

Rome is the largest city in, and the county seat of, Floyd County, Georgia, built on seven hills at the meeting of the Etowah and Oostanaula rivers, which join to form the Coosa River. U.S. Route 27 and U.S. Route 411 run through town, with State Route 20 crossing east and west.

The Clock Tower on Neely Hill — an 1871 water tank turned into the city’s iconic landmark, on the city crest — looks over downtown, and the historic Floyd County Courthouse stands along the Oostanaula. Berry College, whose 27,000-plus acres make it the largest contiguous college campus in the world, sits on the city’s north side.

Broad Street downtown and the riverwalk follow the rivers through the city, and the Heritage Trail System links downtown to the Etowah, Oostanaula and Coosa. Rome is the largest city near the center of the triangle formed by the interstates between Atlanta, Birmingham and Chattanooga.

We serve businesses across Floyd County, including Cedartown, Silver Creek and Cave Spring, as well as nearby Calhoun. Work is done remotely by phone, email and online project reviews; there is no separate Rome office.
