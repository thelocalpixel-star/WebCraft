Is $500 really the full price?

Yes. Your custom website, up to five pages, mobile layouts, and revisions before launch are included. Extra pages are $50 each. Hosting and care are optional at $20/month; we’ll agree on any extras before starting.

How soon can my website go live?

Typically 5–7 business days from our kickoff call. We aim to share a first draft within 48 hours. The final timing depends on how quickly we receive your content and feedback.

What do I need to get started?

Tell us about your business and what your website needs to do. Bring your logo, photos, and any text you already have. We’ll walk through what’s missing together.

Can you help with my domain and hosting?

Absolutely. We can help with your domain and offer hosting and ongoing care for $20/month. Prefer to host it yourself? You’ll own the files and can take them with you.

What if I want to change the design?

Revisions before launch are included. We’ll refine the design with you so the finished website feels right for your business.
