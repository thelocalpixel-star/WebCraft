What a local SEO quote depends on

There is no fixed price for local SEO because the work depends on four things: what you sell, where you compete, what you already have, and how much ongoing work you want.

Scope comes first. A business with one service in one town needs less than a business with five services across twenty towns. The quote follows the number of service pages, the locations that genuinely matter, and the pages that need rewriting rather than a quick pass.

Market comes second. Cleveland and Chattanooga are different searches with different competitors. A town with a few established providers costs less to compete in than a city where every result is held by firms with years of reviews, citations, and content behind them. That is why the quote is made around your market, not from a fixed menu.

Starting point comes third. A site that is already fast, already indexed, and already has accurate business details needs less foundation work than a site that is not indexed, has broken pages, or lists old information in directories. The audit finds that before any price is set.

Ongoing work is the last part. A one-time cleanup fixes what is wrong today. Monthly work keeps details current, answers reviews, and adds pages that answer buyer questions. You choose one or both, and the quote says which. See how we check local search visibility in Cleveland and what the map pack shows before deciding.

Local SEO: questions before you start

Can you guarantee first place on Google?

No. Search results depend on relevance, competition, proximity and other factors outside one provider’s control. We agree on the work and reporting, not a guaranteed position or inquiry volume.

Do I need a separate page for every town?

Only when a page can answer a distinct customer need with useful information. A clear service-area directory and detailed service pages are often a better starting point than many nearly identical city pages.

Can a remote business have a Google Business Profile?

Eligibility depends on how the business serves customers. Online-only businesses are generally ineligible. Businesses that meet customers in person may qualify under Google’s rules; a service-area listing does not justify a fictional storefront.

What will local SEO cost?

We quote around your website, market and agreed deliverables. Setup work and ongoing reviews are scoped before starting. Local SEO is separate from the $500 website package and from advertising spend.

How much does local SEO cost per month?

It depends on scope, market, starting point and ongoing work, so WebCraft quotes around your market rather than from a fixed menu. Published retainers from other firms give the range: Candor in Cleveland $399 a month plus a $499 startup fee, Nine by Six in Maryville from $399 a month, SEOteric in Chattanooga from $750 a month, SEO Tech Pro in Chattanooga $497, $997 or $1,797 a month — as published on their sites in November 2026. Expect months, not days: most businesses see meaningful movement in 3 to 6 months, and no honest provider guarantees a number-one ranking.

Why is there no fixed price for local SEO?

Because the work is not the same for every business. Scope, market, starting point, and ongoing work all change the price, so a fixed number would either overcharge a small job or underprice a large one. The quote is made after the audit, around your market and the deliverables agreed.

How long does local SEO take to show results?

Months, not days. Search engines need time to crawl new pages, and local results depend on reviews, citations, and content built up over time. Anyone promising a top position in a fixed number of days is selling a guarantee no one can keep. We report on indexing, queries, and inquiries as they come in, so you can see whether the work is moving.

How do I know local SEO is working?

By the inquiries it brings: calls, form submissions and bookings you can count, and the search queries bringing visitors to your site. Positions move daily and vary by searcher location, so a ranking screenshot is not the measure — a month with more real inquiries than the month before it is.

Will local SEO help my business show up in AI answers?

It helps in the same way it helps in search. AI systems quote pages that state facts clearly, answer the question directly, and carry structured data they can read. We build pages that way — clear service pages, consistent business details, and schema on every page. No one can guarantee a citation in an AI answer, and we do not promise one.

Is AI search replacing Google?

No. People still use Google for most local searches, and AI answers sit on top of the same web pages. The work that ranks a page — clear content, honest details, good structure — is the same work that makes it quotable in an AI answer. We do both together, not one instead of the other.

How you will know local SEO is working

The measure of local SEO is inquiries, not positions. Calls, form submissions and bookings are things a business can count in its own records. A position on page one that brings no calls is not working; a listing further down the page that brings two calls a week is. Positions move daily and vary by where the searcher is standing, so they are context, not the result — and any report that counts its own work as the result is not a report worth reading.

What to count each month is short: answered calls from search, form submissions that name the service and the town, direction requests, and the search queries bringing visitors to the site. What to ignore is shorter still: a single ranking screenshot, total visits with no inquiries attached, and any promise of a fixed position in a fixed number of days. A month with more real inquiries than the month before it is local SEO working. A month with a better screenshot and the same number of calls is not.
