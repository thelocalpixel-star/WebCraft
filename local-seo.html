Local SEO: questions before you start

Can you guarantee first place on Google?

No. Search results depend on relevance, competition, proximity and other factors outside one provider’s control. We agree on the work and reporting, not a guaranteed position or inquiry volume.

Do I need a separate page for every town?

Only when a page can answer a distinct customer need with useful information. A clear service-area directory and detailed service pages are often a better starting point than many nearly identical city pages.

Can a remote business have a Google Business Profile?

Eligibility depends on how the business serves customers. Online-only businesses are generally ineligible. Businesses that meet customers in person may qualify under Google’s rules; a service-area listing does not justify a fictional storefront.

What will local SEO cost?

We quote around your website, market and agreed deliverables. Setup work and ongoing reviews are scoped before starting. Local SEO is separate from the $500 website package and from advertising spend.
