Start with the service people need

Write a short section for each major service rather than a long list of buzzwords. Explain what the customer can ask for, what information helps you provide an estimate, and where you work. If an in-person visit matters, make the address and hours easy to find.

Make updates part of the plan

Before approving a site, ask who owns the domain and files, how hosting is paid, and who will update the content. When services or hours change, check your website and the listings you control so customers see consistent information.
