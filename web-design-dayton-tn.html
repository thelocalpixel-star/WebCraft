Serving Dayton and Rhea County

Web design for Dayton businesses, in the seat of Rhea County.

Dayton is the county seat of Rhea County, about 36 miles northeast of Chattanooga. U.S. Route 27 runs north and south through town, State Route 30 runs east and west, and State Route 60 connects Dayton with Cleveland to the southeast.

The 1925 Scopes Trial took place in the Rhea County Courthouse on Market Street — the trial over teaching evolution, with statues of William Jennings Bryan and Clarence Darrow outside. Chickamauga Lake and the Tennessee River sit to the east, and the Laurel-Snow State Natural Area holds waterfalls and the remains of old coal mines.

Watts Bar Nuclear Plant and Watts Bar Dam sit on the Tennessee River near Spring City, just north of Dayton, and Walden Ridge, part of the Cumberland Plateau, forms the county’s western border.

We serve businesses across Rhea County, including Spring City, Decatur and Pikeville, as well as nearby Graysville. Work is done remotely by phone, email and online project reviews; there is no separate Dayton office.
