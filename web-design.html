How your web design project starts

Plan your content

Send your services, business details, photographs you have permission to use and the customer action you want. If you already have a website, share what works and what needs to change.

Review the scope

Agree on pages, content responsibilities, revisions, integrations and a delivery schedule before the build. Review the draft remotely by phone, email and online project reviews.

Prepare for launch

Check mobile layouts, contact paths, page titles and the preferred page URLs. Decide who controls your domain and hosting accounts and how future changes will be handled.

Choose the pages around the job your website needs to do.

For a contractor, that may mean service descriptions, project photographs and a quote request. For a café, customers may need a menu, current hours and directions. We start with those decisions before choosing the layout.

Web design: questions before you start

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. Share the current URL and explain what needs to change. We can discuss a redesign and identify important existing URLs before changing page structure. The scope depends on the content and features you need.

Is local SEO included in the $500 package?

The website package includes search engine basics. Ongoing local SEO, Google Ads management and analytics work are separate services with agreed scopes. A website launch does not guarantee indexing, rankings or inquiries.

Who supplies the text and photos?

Bring your accurate business information and any text or photographs you already own. We agree on writing, image sourcing and permissions as part of the scope. The design examples on this site are fictional concepts, not customer projects.

How long does a project take?

Timing depends on the pages, content readiness, feedback and integrations. We agree on a schedule with you before work begins rather than promise the same turnaround for every business.
